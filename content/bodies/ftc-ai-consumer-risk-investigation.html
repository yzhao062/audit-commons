What Is Confirmed

AP reported on September 30 that the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) was investigating OpenAI, Anthropic, and other AI companies over potential consumer risks. An FTC spokesperson confirmed the investigation but declined further comment. AP said the New York Post first reported the inquiry and described it as underway for months.

What Readers Should Look For Next

This brief relies on AP's reporting; we have not verified a public FTC case document. The confirmation alone does not establish the legal basis, document demands, or outcome. Those questions require further evidence.

Our editorial recommendation is to distinguish investigation notices, allegations, and final findings when following this story. Each supports a different claim. The voluntary White House accord is a separate development. The reported timeline places the inquiry before the accord.
