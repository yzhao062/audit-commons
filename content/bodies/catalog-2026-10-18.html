The October 18 refresh brings our resource catalog to 213 entries, with one addition and no revisions or removals to existing records.

  • Praesidia audit verifier: a command-line tool, built from source, that checks exported Praesidia decision-audit bundles offline, including record hashes, Ed25519 signatures, hash chains, and Merkle proofs. Its public samples use test keys, so trust in a production bundle has to be established separately. Project.

The entry arrived as a pull request to the companion list from a contributor who disclosed an affiliation with Praesidia, the company whose export format the tool checks. Before merging, our review built the verifier from source and ran its test suite and sample checks. That review covers the listed functions; it does not assess Praesidia's hosted platform or the origin of any production key.
