Two Uses of AI, Retained Auditor Responsibility

The AICPA's October 13 announcement introduces a practice aid for financial statement auditors. One part addresses company AI use in reporting and controls; another addresses audit firms' own tools. The public release discusses data quality, model risks, team skills, supervision, and human review. Auditors retain responsibility for conclusions. More guidance on evidence and material misstatement is planned.

Evidence to Preserve

This brief summarizes the public announcement. The full practice aid requires member access. Our editorial audit questions are: which company records support a claim, which inputs and outputs came from the auditor's own AI tool, and who reviewed the resulting judgement?

Model and tool versions, source records, and human sign-off can help answer those questions. Our agent action worksheet offers a related evidence exercise. The independently authored FAIAS framework provides another financial AI resource.
