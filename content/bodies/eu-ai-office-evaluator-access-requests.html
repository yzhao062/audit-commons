Reported Information Requests and Advisory Scope

In an October 12 readout updated October 13 on an October 9 meeting, the European Commission reported that its AI Office and AI Scientific Panel reviewed agent loss-of-control incidents and model monitorability. In October 9 remarks published October 13, Executive Vice-President Henna Virkkunen cited over 20 documented 2026 loss-of-control incidents across developers and evaluators, and reported initial Requests for Information covering containment security, independent external evaluator access, and post-market monitoring.

Per the readout, the Commission and panel prepared combined follow-up incident questions, while the panel offered advisory recommendations on internal testing governance, investigation capacity, and monitorability scrutiny. The linked public materials do not include the questionnaires or provider responses and establish no compliance verdict.
