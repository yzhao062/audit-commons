Coverage runs from November 16 through November 21, 2026, Pacific time. Eight bounded Agy discovery units surveyed lab disclosures, government action, independent evaluators, standards and assurance, papers, agent security, follow-ups to existing stories, and the companion ecosystem site. A small Claude Workflow drafted six bilingual news briefs from the primary sources, one agent per brief. Six Agy units then checked every draft and its Chinese edition against those sources, six more searched for licensed lead images, and two reviewed the open pull requests to Awesome Auditable AI. The coordinator integrated the batch and the catalog changes.

Fact-checking changed every brief, and three findings corrected errors. The IETF draft said that no minutes had been posted, but the session page carried minutes from November 21 that reproduce the working notes; the brief now says so and states that the reading poll asked about all four drafts.
