## Selection and Rights Notes

Each image is a results figure or a substantive passage that the brief discusses. Decorative header art on the Anthropic and ARI pages was rejected. Captions identify screenshots as original-source screenshots with capture dates, carry each source's evidence limits, and contain no AI label.

The Redwood and AISI images come from the papers rather than the blog posts because arXiv lists both papers under CC BY 4.0, while neither website states a license. Redwood's October 24 blog shows a near-identical chart whose side-task and environment values differ slightly from the paper, for example 25.05 against 24.70 for BashArena across side-task categories. The pooled 3.57 and 10.10 cited by the brief match in both.

No reuse license is inferred for the Anthropic, OpenAI, or ARI images. `license_pending` is not set because no license is being sought, so structured data lists the linked terms page as the image license, as for other entries without an open license. No asset reproduces exploit steps or biological methods. The AISI crop omits the paper's cost panel, consistent with the brief's omission of run budgets. Source bytes, page captures, rendering parameters, and digests were retained as session evidence; the registry records each asset's digest and changes.

## Policy Change

The weekly and ecosystem refresh skills, [Editorial images](../media.md), and the [maintenance playbook](../maintenance.md) now require real imagery for news leads: source figures, genuine photographs, or labeled original-source screenshots. AI-generated or other generated illustrations are no longer a default or fallback. They need the user's explicit request or approval for that article and disclosure in both language captions; otherwise the editor keeps searching sources and reports the missing real image.
